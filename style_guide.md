# Visual Style Guide

## Illustration style
- Soft editorial illustration, gouache texture
- Simple, elegant forms; no clutter
- One clear subject per slide

## Colors
- Warm, muted, earthy palette
- Soft natural light

## Layout
- Vertical 4:5 (Instagram portrait)
- Leave clean empty space for text:
  - Arabic slides: upper RIGHT
  - English slides: upper LEFT
- Main subject in the opposite lower corner

## Strict rules
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, or symbols in the image
- Same style on every slide of a carousel# Visual Style Guide

## Scenes
- One clear subject per slide, simple and uncluttered
- Show people and everyday objects, not abstract shapes
- Calm, thoughtful, human mood
- Keep the main subject in the lower part of the image

## Strict rules
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, or symbols in the image
